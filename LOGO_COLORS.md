# RestaurFy Logo Color Palette

## Primary Colors
- **Vibrant Orange**: `#FF6B35` (Primary accent)
- **Warm Orange**: `#FF8C42` (Gradient middle)
- **Golden Orange**: `#F7931E` (Gradient end)

## Why These Colors?
- **Appetizing**: Orange is associated with food, warmth, and appetite
- **Modern**: Vibrant gradients create a contemporary, tech-forward feel
- **Professional**: Balanced saturation maintains business credibility
- **Versatile**: Works on both light and dark backgrounds

## Usage
- Logo icon: Use full gradient
- Text accent: Use `#FF6B35` for "Fy" in "RestaurFy"
- Backgrounds: Use subtle tints (10-20% opacity)
- CTAs: Use solid `#FF6B35` for buttons

## Alternative Color Options
If you want to experiment:
- **Red-Orange**: `#FF5722` to `#FF7043`
- **Coral**: `#FF6F61` to `#FF8A65`
- **Amber**: `#FFB300` to `#FFC107`
